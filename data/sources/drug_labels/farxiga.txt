--- DOCUMENT: Farxiga (dapagliflozin) | Therapeutic Area: Diabetes ---
BRAND: Farxiga
GENERIC: dapagliflozin
CLASS: SGLT2 inhibitor
INDICATIONS: Glycemic control adjunct in adults with type 2 diabetes, with an expanded label covering a broader range of renal function stages than most SGLT2 competitors.
DOSAGE: 5 mg orally once daily; StepUp titration to 10 mg after a 3-week tolerability window.
WARNINGS: Rare interaction noted with high-potassium diets. Caution advised for patients on Class-R diuretics. Standard SGLT2-class monitoring applies.
COMPARED TO Jardiance: Farxiga's broader renal-stage coverage is the primary reason nephrology-adjacent accounts prefer it, while Jardiance remains easier to position in primary care due to its simpler dosing card.
STRENGTHS: Internal real-world adherence data suggests roughly 12% better adherence in a 6-month cohort. Renal-stage flexibility supports use in a wider patient population.
WEAKNESSES: Higher rate of mild GI complaints reported in post-market surveillance summaries. Titration window adds a step to initial patient counseling.
