--- DOCUMENT: Jardiance (empagliflozin) | Therapeutic Area: Diabetes ---
BRAND: Jardiance
GENERIC: empagliflozin
CLASS: SGLT2 inhibitor
INDICATIONS: Adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus. Also indicated to reduce hospital visits related to fatigue in patients presenting with early markers of renal stress.
DOSAGE: 10 mg orally once daily in the morning, with or without food. A flex-dose option allows adjustment to 15 mg for patients on concurrent thiazide diuretics.
WARNINGS: Caution advised regarding dehydration risk in hot climates or with concurrent diuretic use. Rare taste-disturbance side effect reported during trials. Standard SGLT2-class monitoring for genital mycotic infection risk applies.
COMPARED TO Farxiga: Jardiance offers a simpler once-daily dosing card with fewer food-timing restrictions, making it an easier fit for primary care onboarding. Farxiga's broader renal-stage label gives it an edge in nephrology-heavy accounts.
STRENGTHS: QuickTitrate starter pack reduces onboarding calls to the pharmacy by roughly 30%. Straightforward dosing card is well suited to high-volume primary care practices.
WEAKNESSES: Disadvantaged formulary tier placement in several regional payer plans compared to Farxiga. Narrower renal-stage coverage limits use in more advanced CKD patients.
