--- DOCUMENT: Trelegy Ellipta (fluticasone/umeclidinium/vilanterol) | Therapeutic Area: Asthma ---
BRAND: Trelegy Ellipta
GENERIC: fluticasone furoate/umeclidinium/vilanterol
CLASS: ICS/LAMA/LABA triple combination
INDICATIONS: Maintenance treatment of asthma in adults, including use during a seasonal flare-prevention phase.
DOSAGE: One inhalation once daily via the EasyBreathe inhaler mechanism.
WARNINGS: Mild voice hoarseness reported in a subset of patients during post-launch monitoring.
COMPARED TO Fasenra: Trelegy Ellipta is the simpler daily-inhaler option for the broader asthma population, while Fasenra is preferred for a narrower, more severe high-eosinophil subtype.
STRENGTHS: Single device, once daily — reduces technique-consistency issues for patients managing multiple inhalers.
WEAKNESSES: Narrower clinical rationale for the more severe eosinophilic subtype where Fasenra is often preferred.
