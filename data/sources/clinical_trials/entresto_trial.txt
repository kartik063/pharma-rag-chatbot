--- TRIAL: PARADIGM-RECOVERPHASE | Drug: Entresto (sacubitril/valsartan) ---
NCT ID: NCT-MOCK-00777
PHASE: Phase 3
CONDITION: Post-recovery maintenance phase of chronic heart failure
DESIGN: Randomized, double-blind, active-controlled versus Cardiphex
SUMMARY: Compared sacubitril/valsartan to Cardiphex specifically in a post-recovery maintenance patient population.
KEY RESULT: Substantial reduction in a composite hospitalization endpoint versus Cardiphex.
REP TALKING POINT: Central "landmark outcomes" data point used when positioning Entresto against Cardiphex.
