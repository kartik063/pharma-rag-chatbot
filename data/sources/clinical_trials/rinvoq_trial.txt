--- TRIAL: MEASUREUP-RAPIDITCH | Drug: Rinvoq (upadacitinib) ---
NCT ID: NCT-MOCK-00555
PHASE: Phase 3
CONDITION: Rapid-onset itch subtype of atopic dermatitis
DESIGN: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, approximately 1,200-patient cohort
SUMMARY: Evaluated upadacitinib's itch-relief onset speed in a patient population specifically presenting with a rapid-onset itch subtype.
KEY RESULT: Itch improvement reported as early as Day 2 versus placebo.
REP TALKING POINT: Central "Day 2 itch relief" claim used to differentiate Rinvoq from Dupixent.
