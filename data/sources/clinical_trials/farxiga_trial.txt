--- TRIAL: DAPA-RENALSCOPE | Drug: Farxiga (dapagliflozin) ---
NCT ID: NCT-MOCK-00222
PHASE: Phase 3
CONDITION: Broader renal-stage population, with and without diabetes
DESIGN: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, approximately 3,500-patient cohort
SUMMARY: Tested dapagliflozin across a wider range of renal function stages than typical SGLT2 trials, including patients without a diabetes diagnosis.
KEY RESULT: Consistent benefit shown across all renal-stage subgroups studied, supporting the drug's broad renal-stage positioning.
REP TALKING POINT: Key reference point for nephrology conversations, supporting Farxiga's expanded renal-stage positioning relative to Jardiance.
