--- TRIAL: DAPA-HF | Drug: Farxiga (dapagliflozin) ---
NCT ID: NCT03036124
PHASE: Phase 3
CONDITION: Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction, with or without type 2 diabetes
DESIGN: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of 4,744 patients with symptomatic heart failure and left ventricular ejection fraction of 40% or less
PRIMARY ENDPOINT: Composite of worsening heart failure, defined as hospitalization or an urgent heart failure visit requiring intravenous therapy, or cardiovascular death
KEY RESULT: The primary endpoint occurred in 16.3% of patients receiving dapagliflozin and 21.2% receiving placebo, corresponding to a 26% relative risk reduction (hazard ratio 0.74; 95% CI 0.65-0.85; P<0.001).
HEART FAILURE HOSPITALIZATION: Worsening heart failure occurred in 10.0% of dapagliflozin patients versus 13.7% of placebo patients (hazard ratio 0.70; 95% CI 0.59-0.83).
CARDIOVASCULAR DEATH: Cardiovascular death occurred in 9.6% of dapagliflozin patients versus 11.5% of placebo patients (hazard ratio 0.82; 95% CI 0.69-0.98).
CONSISTENCY: The benefit on the primary endpoint was consistent in patients with and without type 2 diabetes.
REP TALKING POINT: DAPA-HF supports discussing dapagliflozin as an add-on therapy for eligible patients with HFrEF, independent of diabetes status.
