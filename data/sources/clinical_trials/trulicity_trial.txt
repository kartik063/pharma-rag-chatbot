--- TRIAL: AWARD-STEPUP | Drug: Trulicity (dulaglutide) ---
NCT ID: NCT-MOCK-00333
PHASE: Phase 3 program
CONDITION: Type 2 Diabetes, multi-arm comparator design
SUMMARY: Pooled trial program comparing dulaglutide against Semaglor and an oral comparator across several treatment arms, including a pen-training sub-study.
KEY RESULT: Statistically significant A1c reduction versus Semaglor, with a notably reduced pen-training time reported in the sub-study.
REP TALKING POINT: Supports the "single-click pen" convenience claim used in prescriber conversations.
